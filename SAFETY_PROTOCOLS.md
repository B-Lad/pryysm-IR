# Safety Protocols for Prysm 3D Printing System

## Overview
This document outlines critical safety protocols for operating the Prysm industrial 3D printing system.

## Emergency Procedures

### 1. Emergency Stop
- Locate the red E-STOP button on each printer
- Press firmly in case of any emergency
- System will immediately halt all operations

### 2. Fire Safety
- Never leave printers unattended during high-temperature operations
- Keep fire extinguishers rated for electrical fires nearby
- Ensure proper ventilation in printing areas

### 3. Material Handling
- Wear appropriate PPE when handling resins and powders
- Store materials in climate-controlled environments
- Follow MSDS guidelines for all chemicals

## Operational Safety

### Pre-Print Checklist
- [ ] Verify all safety interlocks are engaged
- [ ] Check ventilation systems
- [ ] Inspect power connections
- [ ] Confirm emergency stops are functional

### During Print Monitoring
- Monitor temperature readings continuously
- Watch for unusual sounds or vibrations
- Check for material leaks or spills

### Post-Print Procedures
- Allow proper cooling time before handling prints
- Follow proper waste disposal protocols
- Document any anomalies or incidents

## Maintenance Safety
- Lock out/tag out procedures must be followed
- Only trained personnel should perform maintenance
- Keep maintenance logs up to date
